# Portfolio Content Rules

Truthfulness and professional ethics take priority over promotional language.

## Never invent

- Clients or brands
- Metrics, percentages, revenue, or campaign performance
- Results or business outcomes
- Education or job titles
- Certifications
- Awards
- Testimonials
- Years, achievements, or ownership claims not supplied by the user

If information is unavailable, use neutral factual language or leave it out. Do not manufacture placeholder facts.

## Public-safe work only

Publish only work that is already public or explicitly approved for portfolio use, such as published websites, public social posts, customer-facing campaigns, brochures, catalogues, company profiles, landing pages, and product communication.

Professional employment work must be attributed appropriately. Treewalker work should be described as work created as part of Shubham's professional role, not as a freelance client relationship.

## Never expose

- Passwords, API credentials, tokens, or private links
- Source code from employers
- Private dashboards or customer records
- Internal financial information or business strategy
- Confidential or unreleased documents and projects
- Large editable source files such as PSD, AI, or working Figma exports

Before publishing a new asset, confirm that it is public-safe, optimized for the web, and free of unintended private information.
