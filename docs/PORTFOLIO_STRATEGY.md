# Portfolio Strategy

## Professional positioning

**Shubham Kumar — Brand & Marketing Visual Designer**

Shubham is positioned as a business-focused visual communication professional who understands brands, products, audiences, marketing objectives, technology, customer communication, and execution across channels.

Do not narrow the positioning to only graphic design, social media execution, Canva work, or artwork production.

## Portfolio objectives

1. Support strong full-time career opportunities.
2. Attract serious freelance clients.

The same portfolio must serve both goals without becoming vague or splitting into conflicting identities.

## What the portfolio must communicate

- Business understanding
- Marketing understanding
- Brand and visual communication
- Technology and product understanding
- Clear customer communication
- Strategic thinking and reliable execution
- Consistency across digital, motion, presentation, campaign, web, and print touchpoints

## Storytelling principle

Case studies should follow this sequence when evidence is available:

**Business Context → Communication Challenge → Thinking / Approach → Visual System → Execution → Evidence / Reflection**

Start with the business or communication need, not a list of deliverables. Show how complex products, services, or ideas were made easier to understand, trust, and remember.

## Work selection

The portfolio is not a gallery of everything Shubham has designed. Select fewer, stronger examples that demonstrate range, business judgment, technology familiarity, visual quality, and cross-channel consistency.

Use real public-safe work. Attribute employment work accurately and never imply ownership of an employer's products or business results.
